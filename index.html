Volunteer with Santa Clara Law

Judge the Moot Court Competition

We're looking for attorneys, alumni, and faculty to serve as judges for oral arguments. No prior moot court experience is required — we provide the bench memo and scoring rubric in advance.

Express interest →
Commitment

One round, ~2 hours

Judges are assigned to a single round of oral arguments and typically serve on a three-judge panel.

What you'll do

Score and question

Ask questions during oral argument and score each competitor using the provided rubric.

No account needed

Simple sign-up

Express interest through the form below — a member of the Moot Court Board will follow up with logistics by email.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Judges do not need to log in anywhere on this site. Fill out the interest form, and our team will email you directly with your assignment, room, and materials.

What if I've never judged moot court before?

That's fine — we'll send a short scoring guide and bench memo ahead of your round, and a member of the Moot Court Board will be on hand to answer questions.

How are scores used?

Each judge scores independently. Competitors are ranked using the mean and mode of their panel's three scores, with a documented tie-break rule if needed.
