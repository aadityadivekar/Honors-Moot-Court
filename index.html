Competitor Rules and Guidelines
Full competition rules and guidelines will be posted here. Contact the Moot Court Board at hmc@scu.edu with any questions in the meantime.

Who's eligible?

Post-grad JDs, practicing lawyers, post-bar attorneys, and legal academia — we're interested in having a variety of perspectives for our competitions.

What is the competition format?

Each round, Petitioner and Respondent teams of two will present arguments around two issues, with each speaker arguing for 15 minutes, and the Petitioner team given a chance for rebuttal. The judges will then confer on individual scores amongst themselves, and call the advocates for general or specific feedback.

What are event schedules like?

We ask that judges arrive 15 minutes prior to the start of the round to ensure no delays. Rounds will typically last 75–90 minutes, with an allotted break time. Two rounds will occur each competition date — please let us know how many rounds you'd be interested in judging.

Where can I park?

Santa Clara Law has two adjacent parking structures, each one block from Charney Hall. During the weekdays, parking is paid, but please email hmc@scu.edu to reserve a parking day-pass. Parking is free on weekends. Charney Hall is fully ADA-accessible.

Is a bench brief provided beforehand?

Yes! The HMC Board will send you a copy of the bench brief, along with a few opportunities to consult the HMC Board with any questions.

If something comes up, how should I cancel?

Please email hmc@scu.edu with any cancellations as soon as you know of your conflict.
